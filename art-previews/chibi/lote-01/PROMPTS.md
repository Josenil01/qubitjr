Image 2 is the ORIGINAL SUBJECT: preserve its species and identifying colors/details but redraw fully in the cow master's chibi style. No realistic fur strokes, no intricate texture, no 3D rendering. Friendly three-quarter view facing slightly left (frog may face front). Complete anatomy, all extremities visible, comfortably centered, consistent generous transparent margins, subject occupies about 80-85% of square canvas. True transparent alpha background.
